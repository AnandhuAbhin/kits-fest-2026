Solo Song - Rules & Regulations

  • Time limit for each performance is strictly 3 to 5 minutes. Negative points for exceeding the time limit.
  • Participants can perform songs in any language. Obscene or offensive lyrics will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • Karaoke tracks are allowed but must be submitted to the event coordinators at least 2 hours before the event starts.
  • Singing along with vocal tracks is strictly prohibited; only instrumental/karaoke tracks are allowed.
  • Live accompaniment (maximum one person playing an instrument) is allowed, but the accompanist will not be judged.
  • Judging criteria: Voice Quality, Rhythm and Pitch accuracy, Song selection, and Overall Stage Presence.
  • Registration fee is ₹100/- and is non-refundable under any circumstances.
  • The decision of the judges and organizing committee will be final and binding.
